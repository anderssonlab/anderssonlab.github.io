<h3>Thesis projects</h3>

Interested in carrying out a Bioinformatics project or M.Sc. thesis in the Andersson Lab? Please send Robin an <a href="mailto:robin@bio.ku.dk">email</a> to arrange a meeting and discuss possible projects.

Example project areas include:

<ul>
<li>Machine learning models of gene regulation and regulatory DNA sequence</li>
<li>Computational methods for identifying active regulatory elements from transcription initiation data</li>
<li>Computational methods for mapping enhancer–gene interactions from single-cell genomics data</li>
<li>Atlas-scale analysis of regulatory elements across cell types and biological systems</li>
<li>Variant-to-function prediction and interpretation of noncoding genetic variation</li>
</ul>

Students are encouraged to develop independent research projects while working closely with members of the lab. Projects typically combine method development with applications to large-scale genomics datasets.
