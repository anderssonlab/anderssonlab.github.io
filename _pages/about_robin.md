Robin Andersson is Associate Professor of Bioinformatics and Deputy Head of Department for Research at the Department of Biology, University of Copenhagen. His research focuses on regulatory genomics, combining experimental genomics, computational biology, and machine learning to understand how noncoding DNA regulates gene expression and contributes to human disease. <!-- His laboratory develops methods for regulatory genomics to identify active regulatory elements, predict enhancer-gene interactions, uncover the sequence logic of gene regulation, and interpret the functional consequences of noncoding genetic variation. -->

Robin received his M.Sc. in Computer Science from Linköping University (Sweden) in 2004 and his Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Uppsala University (Sweden) in 2010. He subsequently joined the laboratory of Prof. Albin Sandelin at the University of Copenhagen as a postdoctoral researcher, where he investigated transcriptional regulation and enhancer biology. In 2015, he was awarded a European Research Council (ERC) Starting Grant, which enabled him to establish the Andersson Lab at the Department of Biology, University of Copenhagen.

Robin became a tenured Associate Professor of Bioinformatics in 2020. He served as Head of the Section for Computational and RNA Biology from 2024 to 2026 and was appointed Deputy Head of Department for Research in 2026. His group’s research has advanced our understanding of enhancers, promoters, and transcription initiation, and has led to widely used experimental and computational approaches for mapping regulatory elements, linking enhancers to their target genes, and interpreting noncoding genetic variation.
