<h3>PRIME predictions</h3>
Genome-wide predictions of active regulatory elements generated using PRIME across <b>141 FANTOM5 cell type and tissue facets</b>.
<ul>
<li>Paper: Einarsson H, et al. <a href="https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.11.724207">Mapping active cis-regulatory elements from transcription initiation events</a>. bioRxiv. 2026.</li>
<li>Interactive browser: <a href="https://anderssonlab.org/PRIMEatlas/">PRIME Atlas</a></li>
<li>Download: <a href="https://zenodo.org/records/19712783">PRIME predictions</a></li>
</ul>
<hr>

<h3>Enhancer–gene regulatory interactions</h3>
Genome-wide scE2G predictions of enhancer–gene regulatory interactions across seven 10x Genomics multiome datasets, including K562, GM12878, PBMC, BMMC, and pancreatic islets.
<ul>
<li>Paper: Sheth, Qiu, et al. <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-026-02695-8">Mapping enhancer–gene regulatory interactions from single-cell data</a>. Nature Genetics. 2026.</li>
<li>Download: <a href="https://data.broadinstitute.org/scE2Gpredictions/">scE2G predictions</a> (files named <code>[Dataset]/scE2G/scE2G_multiome.[CellType].predictions.tsv.gz</code>)</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>FANTOM5 enhancers</h3>
Divergently transcribed enhancers identified from CAGE data generated by the <a href="https://fantom.gsc.riken.jp/">FANTOM</a> consortium.
<ul>
<li>Paper: Andersson R, et al. <a href="http://dx.doi.org/10.1038/nature12787">An atlas of active enhancers across human cell types and tissues</a>. Nature. 2014.</li>
<li><a href="https://fantom.gsc.riken.jp/5/datafiles/latest/extra/Enhancers/">Original</a> data (hg19 and mm9)</li>
<li>Reprocessed human CAGE data aligned to <a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.556775">GRCh38 (hg38)</a></li>
<li>Reprocessed mouse CAGE data aligned to <a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1411211">GRCm38 (mm10)</a></li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3>CAGE data across 108 lymphoblastoid cell lines</h3>
CAGE profiles from 108 Epstein–Barr virus-transformed lymphoblastoid cell lines (89 YRI and 19 LWK) generated to investigate promoter architecture and regulatory variation.
<ul>
<li>Paper: Einarsson H, et al. <a href="https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.80943">Promoter sequence and architecture determine expression variability and confer robustness to genetic variants</a>. eLife. 2022.</li>
<li>Data: NCBI Gene Expression Omnibus <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE188131">GSE188131</a></li>
</ul>
