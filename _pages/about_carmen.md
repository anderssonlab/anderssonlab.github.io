Carmen holds a B.Sc. in Biotechnology (Biomedicine specialization) from the University of Cadiz, Spain (2024), and an M.Sc. in Bioinformatics from the University of Copenhagen (2026).

Her research focuses on understanding gene regulation through the integration of single-cell multi-omics data. Currently, she develops computational methods to investigate how enhancers collectively regulate coordinated gene expression programs, with the aim of improving the interpretation of gene regulatory networks. This research direction originated from her Master's thesis project in Andersson's group.
