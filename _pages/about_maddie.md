Maddie Murphy holds a Bachelor's degree in Computational and Systems Biology and a Master's degree in Bioinformatics from University of California Los Angeles (UCLA). She previously worked as a computational associate at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, where she primarily worked on discovering eQTLs through village studies and analyzing perturb-seq data. Her PhD work focuses on modelling enhancer activity and enhnacer-to-gene regulatory interactions in single cells.
